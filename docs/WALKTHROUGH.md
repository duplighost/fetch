# FETCH — Complete Walkthrough (Designer's Copy)

Everything that exists in the build right now, act by act — every puzzle, every
solution, every enemy rule, and every **THIN** flag marking where the game is
waiting for your puzzle work. Total spoilers; this is the workshop copy.

**The verb, everywhere:** press LMB = throw (it flies where you look). Keep
holding = it stays out, hovers, follows your gaze. Release = it zips back.
Tap RMB or E = call it (backup). Hitting a fetchable thing = it grabs it in
its teeth (clack + chime + spin). E = use doors/hatches. The skull is also
your light — thrown, your light goes with it. Jaw-chatter = something near;
after it grows eyes, they look AT what's hunting you.

---

## ACT 0 — The Bedroom (upstairs, southeast corner)

You wake holding it, facing the open window. Your door is locked from the
inside. Through the window: a tree branch, and a key hanging from it on a
string, swaying.

- **Puzzle 1:** throw the skull out the window at the key. Contact = grab.
  It comes back with the key in its teeth.
- **Puzzle 2:** throw the key-carrying skull at the door (or stand at the
  door and press E while it holds the key). The lock takes the key; the door
  swings open a beat later.

*Teaches: throw, fetch, the tether, "locks are targets." One room, whole game.*

## ACT 1 — First Floor (landing + nursery)

The landing has a candle table and the STAIR DOOR down — locked. The nursery
door hangs ajar (west).

- **The nursery:** the mobile above the crib turns with no wind. The stair
  key hangs FROM it, spinning with the toys. The mobile winds down over about
  a minute — while it's stopped and you're in the room, something in the
  corner by the crib grows. Hit the mobile with the skull to spin it back up
  and the shape recedes. Let it sit at zero too long and the thing finishes
  growing — it rises from the floor and comes (this is the floor-rise you saw).
- **Puzzle:** take the key off the mobile with a throw; throw it at the stair
  door. Down you go.
- Ambient layer up here and below: your own footsteps echoed a half-beat
  behind you (the offset shrinks act by act, all game), knocks from rooms
  away, whispers, the nearest closed door drifting open, footsteps pacing
  the floor you're NOT on.
- **THIN:** the guest room is empty scare-space; the landing has no beat of
  its own. Prime real estate.

## Ground Floor

Entry → foyer → dining/living/study/kitchen. The front door rattles forever.

- **The cellar door** (kitchen) is nailed over with **three boards**. Each
  board = one skull hit. Every break is LOUD —
- **THE RESIDENT** comes on the first break. Big. Cannot be killed, only
  stunned (~1.6s, one hit). It stalks the ground floor and every loud thing
  you do re-agitates it. The play: stun it to buy space, keep breaking.
- Descend the cellar stairs and it does NOT follow — the door slams behind
  you by itself. (Rule taught: doors mean something.)
- **THIN:** living, dining, and study are furnished but purposeless — no
  reason to enter, nothing to solve. This is where your window puzzles, the
  telephone, the portrait hall, the dining-room beats belong. Biggest open
  canvas in the game.

## ACT 2 — The Basement

- East corridor hung with webs (they tear across your face, muffle as you
  pass). The cellar shaft drops you at the east end.
- **The storeroom:** four dropcloth shapes in mid-lunge poses. **One is
  real** — chosen fresh every boot, so even you can't know which. It's a
  Standing One: it crosses the room only while you aren't looking, silently.
- **The boiler room** (heavy shut door, E to open): pilot light, tank — and
  the HATCH KEY glinting on the tank.
- **The hatchbay** (far west): sloped storm doors, padlocked. Throw the
  key-carrying skull at the padlock → E the hatch → climb out.
- **THIN:** solution is a straight fetch-quest. The dark-eats-sound rule and
  throw-your-light-into-the-black staging want to live here.

## ACT 3 — The Graveyard (backyard)

- The crashed car's headlights are dying in flickers. The bodies are posed
  crawling AWAY from the forest gate — the dead are a compass pointing at
  what they fled, which is where you're going. Headstone rows.
- Three things among the stones: one dormant (wakes if you're loud within
  arm's reach), two Standing Kind (they cross only unobserved, and they
  keep to their territory). Walk calm and watch your back and you pass.
- The skull is afraid here — it refuses long throws, hurries back early,
  huddles.
- The gate in the back fence leads to the trees.
- **THIN:** pure traverse, zero puzzle. Your idea bank already owns this
  ground: the open grave, the tuned stones, the mausoleum. Design at will.

## ACT 4 — The Forest (it seals behind you)

Enter and the path closes ~10m behind you, always. Forward only.

- **~1/5 in — the fallen tree:** blocks the path, three hits to clear.
  Clearing it is heard: a stalker spawns up-path a few seconds later.
- **Halfway — the ravine:** a black gash you cannot cross on foot (falling
  in is death). A rope hangs on the far side: throw the skull at the rope —
  it bites — and YOU are launched across. Another one winds up behind you
  shortly after. (Rule: the rope is one-use.)
- **Combat truth for your design work:** chasers outrun you (5.6 vs your
  4.7 sprint). One hit stuns — quiet, ~3s. Hitting a stunned one POPS it —
  permanent, LOUD, and loud invites up to two more. Stun-and-run is the
  smart play; popping is a purchase.
- **~3/4 — the arena:** the path bulges into open ground and the skull
  SCREAMS without asking you. Three waves (3, then 4, then 4) arrive from
  all directions, staggered footfalls, with dead silence between waves.
  Crowd-control wins: stun whatever's closing, execute the downed in the
  gaps. Afterward: total quiet, and the skull grows almost whole.
- **Near the end — the Kneeler:** something huge kneeling beside the path.
  WALK past it. It only wakes for noise. If you run: it rises, and it
  chases you to the treeline and not one step further.
- **THIN:** wants the eyes-vs-fireflies, the second thrower, the canopy
  grapple chain — all logged, all designable.

## ACT 5 — The Clearing (sincere)

The forest lets you out into the one place that doesn't want you dead —
streams, a pool, drifting motes, the giant waterfall on the cliff. Nothing
hunts here. Nothing ever will.

- The head — whole now — keeps turning in your hands toward the waterfall.
- Throw it through the fall. **It does not come back.** Nothing happens when
  you press the button that always worked. Stepping stones rise from the
  pool, one by one.
- Cross, and walk into the water.

## ACT 6 — The Cave and the Room

- A candlelit tunnel someone prepared. Pressing your old call button does
  nothing — except, sometimes, a candle far ahead flares, or stone shifts
  deep in the rock. It hears you. It can't come.
- The end chamber has a wooden hatch in the ceiling. Climb.
- A bedroom much like the first, wrong in the details: the door leads
  nowhere, the window is shut, there is no tree, and your hands are empty.
  Seven seconds of stillness. Then the walls begin to move — and they're
  mirrors. Your reflection has your body and the bare skull for a head.
  It runs a half-beat behind you; the lag closes as the walls do; in the
  last half-meter, it starts moving BEFORE you do.
- The walls do not stop. Dark. The catch-click you've heard four hundred
  times. Someone else's gasp. Title.

## Where the game most needs Alex (ranked)

   out-one-in-another idea belongs here; the poised hover makes it work).
2. **Graveyard** — zero puzzle content; your idea bank already has three.
3. **Basement** — one fetch; wants its darkness mechanics.
4. **Landing/guest room** — empty scare-space upstairs.
5. **Combat encounters** — only the Resident, the forest chasers, and the
   arena exist. The encounter list between here and "terrifying boss
   battles" is unwritten and it's yours.
